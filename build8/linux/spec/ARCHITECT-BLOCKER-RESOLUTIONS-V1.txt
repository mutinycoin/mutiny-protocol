======================================================================
DECISION 1 — LOST HARDWARE / QUORUM LOSS
======================================================================

DO NOT accept permanent loss of the sole bootstrap TPM as an unrecoverable
Build8 bootstrap condition.

REQUIRE an explicit:

BOOTSTRAP RECOVERY AUTHORITY

but ONLY while the network is formally:

BOOTSTRAP_CENTRALIZED.

This is an explicit temporary centralized recovery authority.

It is NOT ordinary Continuity quorum.

It MUST NOT be described as decentralized consensus.

It MUST NOT survive decentralized Continuity activation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1A. NORMAL QUORUM RULE REMAINS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Normal Build8 Continuity finality remains:

strictly greater than 2/3
of committed eligible Continuity Reputation

with persisted:

propose
prevote
precommit
locking.

Offline committed eligible voters remain in the denominator.

No inactivity timeout.

No automatic voter removal.

No owner signature may normally override Continuity finality.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1B. BOOTSTRAP-ONLY RECOVERY AUTHORITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------

During BOOTSTRAP_CENTRALIZED only, Genesis MUST contain an explicit public
BootstrapRecoveryAuthority.

Use a separate, domain-separated recovery signing key.

It MUST NOT be interchangeable with:

ordinary wallet spending signatures
Continuity TPM voting signatures
node authentication signatures
miner signatures.

It may be deterministically derived from the Build8 recovery mnemonic through
a separate hardened/domain-separated derivation if the established wallet
cryptography permits this safely.

Do not invent custom cryptography.

Its purpose is limited to recovering the physical Continuity voter registry
when normal >2/3 Continuity quorum is impossible during centralized bootstrap
because qualifying bootstrap hardware was permanently lost.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1C. REQUIRED BOOTSTRAP RECOVERY OBJECT
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Define a canonical object equivalent to:

BootstrapRecoveryCertificateV1

It MUST bind at minimum:

NetworkID
GenesisID
ProtocolFingerprint

current canonical height
current canonical tip
current StateRoot

current Continuity registry root

monotonic BootstrapRecoverySequence

old Node lineage identity

lost hardware generation

new physical Node identity

new TPM identity / attestation commitment

new TPM-bound Continuity voting public key

operation being authorized

recovery reason/domain

BootstrapRecoveryAuthority signature.

Canonical encoding and test vectors are mandatory.

Replay across:

network
Genesis
canonical anchor
registry generation
recovery sequence

must fail.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1D. WHAT BOOTSTRAP RECOVERY MAY DO
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Bootstrap recovery may authorize ONLY the minimum registry transition required
to replace permanently lost bootstrap Continuity hardware.

The recovered node lineage MAY preserve, according to the already approved
HardwareRotateV1 policy:

Node admission age
qualified participation history
active Bond amount
Bond age
penalty history
lineage.

It MUST reset:

HardwareContinuity H = 0

hardware generation

TPM voting key

physical attestation state.

The replacement machine MUST pass the full approved physical TPM admission
process.

A software VM/vTPM/cloud host can never be installed through this path as a
weighted Continuity voter.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1E. WHAT BOOTSTRAP RECOVERY MAY NOT DO
----------------------------------------------------------------------

It MUST NOT:

rewrite canonical transaction history

spend UTXOs

mint coins

change transaction validity

change PoW

change reward

change Bonds arbitrarily

erase penalties

change NetworkID

change GenesisID

alter historical blocks

override an existing valid >2/3 Continuity decision

install Vultr/cloud/vTPM as a weighted voter.

It exists only to restore the lost physical voter lineage required to resume
bootstrap Continuity operation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1F. WHEN BOOTSTRAP RECOVERY IS ALLOWED
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The technical annex must define deterministic admissibility.

At minimum require:

network still marked BOOTSTRAP_CENTRALIZED;

normal >2/3 committed Continuity quorum cannot currently authorize the required
hardware recovery;

fresh supervised physical TPM qualification of the replacement machine;

correct BootstrapRecoveryAuthority signature;

exact current canonical anchor;

strictly increasing recovery sequence;

no conflicting previously accepted recovery certificate.

Do not rely on wall-clock inactivity as proof that hardware is lost.

The recovery action is an explicit supervised ceremony.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1G. IRREVERSIBLE DISABLE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

BootstrapRecoveryAuthority MUST become permanently unusable once Build8 reaches
decentralized Continuity activation.

Define:

CONTINUITY_DECENTRALIZED_ACTIVE

only after:

at least4 qualified independent physical Continuity voters exist;

AND

measured committed weight distribution retains strict >2/3 after removal of
each individual voter;

AND

the activation transition itself is certified through ordinary Continuity
consensus.

That transition MUST set an irreversible consensus flag equivalent to:

bootstrap_recovery_enabled = false

Once false:

BootstrapRecoveryCertificateV1 MUST always be invalid.

It can never be re-enabled by:

owner key
wallet seed
operator
software update
ordinary governance.

After decentralized activation, the normal >2/3 trust model is absolute.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1H. POST-BOOTSTRAP QUORUM LOSS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

After CONTINUITY_DECENTRALIZED_ACTIVE:

if permanent hardware loss leaves insufficient committed weight to produce
strict >2/3 quorum:

THE CHAIN HALTS.

No emergency owner override.

No inactivity deletion.

No denominator renormalization.

No cloud substitution.

Recovery requires restoration of sufficient legitimate old-set authority
through already-valid ordinary mechanisms.

If that authority is permanently impossible, the chain remains halted.

This is the explicit post-bootstrap safety model.

======================================================================
DECISION 3 — REPUTATION MAY DECREASE
======================================================================

APPROVE the recommendation:

THE FORMULA CONTROLS.

REMOVE the global claim that total VotingReputation is always monotonic absent
objective penalties or hardware replacement.

The approved formula remains:

P = total qualified ContinuityPeriods

A = admission age in ContinuityPeriods

S = current consecutive qualified ContinuityPeriod streak

H = total qualified ContinuityPeriods on current attested hardware generation

Q = sum for active qualifying Bond lots:

    locked_atoms * (1 + bond_age_in_ContinuityPeriods)

GrossReputation =

    1000*P
    + A
    + 4*S
    + 2*H
    + floor(Q / 10^10)

VotingReputation =

    max(0, GrossReputation - ObjectivePenalties)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3A. HISTORICAL COMPONENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

P:
never decreases.

A:
continues with admitted lineage according to the approved lifecycle rules.

Historical lineage remains cumulative.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3B. DYNAMIC COMPONENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Total reputation MAY decrease because:

S resets after a missed qualification period;

an active qualifying Bond is withdrawn and leaves Q;

HardwareContinuity H resets through an authorized hardware migration;

ObjectivePenalties increase;

other explicitly approved dynamic components cease qualifying.

This is intended.

A streak bonus represents CURRENT continuity.

An active Bond contribution represents CURRENT locked economic commitment.

They are not permanent reputation once their underlying condition is gone.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3C. MISSED PERIOD
----------------------------------------------------------------------

No downtime slashing remains the rule.

Missing a ContinuityPeriod:

does NOT burn Bond;

does NOT subtract an additional penalty;

does NOT erase P;

does NOT erase admission lineage.

But:

S resets according to the canonical streak rule.

Therefore total VotingReputation may decrease.

That is not "slashing."

It is loss of the current continuity-streak component.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3D. BOND WITHDRAWAL
----------------------------------------------------------------------

During the approved unbond notice while the Bond remains canonically locked and
qualifying:

it remains in Q according to the technical annex.

When withdrawal becomes effective and the Bond ceases to be qualifying:

its Q contribution disappears.

Bond withdrawal may therefore reduce VotingReputation.

No high-water clamp.

No ghost Bond reputation after funds unlock.
